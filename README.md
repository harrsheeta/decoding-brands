# Decoding Brands — Asset Vault

A self-contained asset library for Decoding Brands edits: animation prompts, sound effects, music and visuals. Every asset is embedded in `index.html`, so it works offline. Open the file directly or serve it with GitHub Pages.

## Projects
- **GTA VI** ("Why 13 years?", https://youtu.be/at8pz2XJtPg): every animation made for this video, with its thumbnail. Animation cards link to their project.
- **Jet Airways** ("The Fall of Jet Airways", https://youtu.be/zRBjDdCRpZs): Indian Airlines Bad Service, Article → 1993 → Four Planes, Half the Planes a Quarter of the Staff, Mumbai → London, Jet Airways Growth Chart 2009–2018, Oil Prices → Jet Loses IndiGo Profits, Departures Board 2003 → 2007, Air Sahara Blueprint Plan, JetLite: Still Air Sahara Inside. The last four play as YouTube embeds.
- **Meta** (https://youtu.be/WvUH0ooAwOM): prompts 09–17. All nine play as YouTube embeds.
- **Dairy** (prompts 27–32) and **Paparazzi Economy** (prompts 33–41): each plays its video from Google Drive in an embedded player.
- **Inspo**: reference animations from the idk show (prompts 18–24), played as YouTube embeds with a muted preview on hover. Prompts 25 and 26 are saved in `prompts/` until their videos are added.

## Collections
- **Animation Prompts** (filters: Map Animation, Motion Graphics, Timeline Animation, Article Animation, Graph Animation, 1/2/3 Cards, Text Animation): Rockstar Studios World Map, Rockstar → RDR2: 87 Million Sold, Rockstar Money Run, John Wick → iPhone Cards (GTA VI project), GTA Online Spending Graph, GTA V Buy-Play-Finish, Take-Two Annual Report. Each has a preview video, a storyboard and the full prompt.
- **Sound Effects**: Marker Pass, Cut Click, with waveform players.
- **Sound (Music Catalog)**: Think School, IDK Show and Fern tabs, laid out like the music-catalog scan (Fern lists the 36 tracks credited in The iPhone Battery Conspiracy; its 25 Epidemic Sound tracks stream their store preview in the waveform player). Each track has its match status, store link, the videos and timestamps it was heard at, and a waveform player for its evidence clip. You can filter by text, status or video. Think School also has tempo tiles for the suggested tracks.
- **Visuals (brand assets)**: master logo, brand style guide 2024, brand pattern, paper texture, podcast logo intro, end screens, podcast thumbnails (PSD), and After Effects / Illustrator source files. The full files live in `assets/brand/` and download from the vault when it's served from this repo.

The home page has a playable Realtime Piano under Projects (keyboard Z–M and Q–] rows, mouse or touch, with volume).

The player keeps a now-playing bar at the bottom of the page. Keys: Space or K plays/pauses, J and L go to the previous/next track, the arrow keys seek, and / jumps to the filter.

The plain-text prompts are also in `prompts/`.
