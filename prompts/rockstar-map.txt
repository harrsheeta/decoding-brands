STEP 1 – TIMING
Transcribe the base video's audio with faster-whisper (base.en, int8, word_timestamps=True). Decode the audio to 16 kHz mono float32 through an ffmpeg pipe and pass the numpy array in, not the file path. Print every word with its start and end times. Set each event to its trigger word's start time. The zoom may start up to 0.1s after its word.

STEP 2 – BACKGROUND
- Before UI_IN, the base video plays untouched.
- From UI_IN, over 0.45s (ease-in-out), crossfade the video into a blurred olive-green version of itself:
  - blur: downscale to 480x270, Gaussian sigma ~3.2, upscale back;
  - colour: luminance L, normalised as (L-0.08)/0.85 and clamped to 0–1, mapped through a 3-stop ramp: dark (14,26,8) → mid (78,104,34) at L=0.55 → light (212,200,128);
  - final: (ramp*0.92 + blurred original*0.08) * 0.82.
  The result should look like a moody, slightly yellow-olive game pause-menu background, not neon green.
- The video keeps playing underneath for the whole clip.
